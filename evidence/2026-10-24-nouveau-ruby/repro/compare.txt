## Why files differ (scripts/kernel-nouveau-check.py compare_module on every differing .ko)

- 100 GKI modules (system_dlkm): identical except the appended signature. Every GKI build makes a new
  module-signing key (kernel.md: "a fixed module-signing key" is an open item), so vmlinux (its .init.data
  holds the certificate; its build ID follows), bzImage, boot.img and system_dlkm differ too.
- 35 vendor modules: identical exports, CRCs, imports and sections except split .BTF. pahole does not
  produce the same split BTF for them twice; the GKI has MODULE_ALLOW_BTF_MISMATCH=y, so BTF never decides
  whether a module loads. vendor_dlkm.img (and vm/vendor_boot.img, with virtio-gpu) differ for that reason.
- 107 vendor modules are byte-identical, among them nouveau.ko, drm_gpuvm.ko; openbook_lid_guard.ko too.

So the release pins the exact files of the first build (handoff-ANAL-239.json). A rebuild reproduces
nouveau.ko, drm_gpuvm.ko and openbook_lid_guard.ko byte for byte, and every other vendor module
apart from BTF.
